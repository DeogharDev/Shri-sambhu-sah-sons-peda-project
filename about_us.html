Serving the Taste of Baidyanath Dham Since Generations

Welcome to Shri Shambhu Sahu Goods & Sons. Located at the sacred Mandir Ka Purva Darwaza in Deoghar, we have dedicated ourselves to preserving the authentic taste of traditional Baba Dham Peda.

Our pedas are not just sweets; they are an emotion, a prasad, and a symbol of purity. Made daily with the finest quality khoya (milk solids), rich sugar, and a touch of traditional cardamom, every bite connects you to the spiritual essence of Baidyanath Dham.

What started as a humble offering for devotees has now become a trusted name. We take pride in our handmade process, ensuring that no artificial preservatives touch our products.

Premium Quality Guarantee

Every batch is prepared with utmost hygiene and devotion, packed securely to deliver the freshness straight from our kitchen to your doorstep anywhere in India.
