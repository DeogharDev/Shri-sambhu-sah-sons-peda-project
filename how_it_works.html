How to Order

Ordering your favorite Baba Dham Peda is simple. Follow these easy steps to get authentic taste delivered straight to your door.

1

Choose Your Peda

Browse our menu, select your favorite traditional peda, choose the perfect weight, and add it to your cart.

2

Enter Delivery PIN

Provide your complete delivery address and PIN code at checkout to check delivery availability in your area.

3

Select Courier

View estimated delivery dates and shipping charges from our premium courier partners. Choose the one that suits you best.

4

Pay Securely

Complete your payment 100% securely through Razorpay using UPI, Cards, or Net Banking. We ensure your data is safe.

5

We Prepare & Ship

Once your order is accepted, we freshly prepare your peda, pack it carefully, and ship it. You will receive WhatsApp updates!

6

Enjoy Baba Dham Taste

Receive your order at your doorstep. Open the box, feel the aroma, and enjoy the authentic taste of Deoghar!
